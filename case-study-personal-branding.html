AI-Powered Personal Branding Optimisation

Strategically repositioned an executive LinkedIn profile through keyword optimisation, profile restructuring, and content positioning, transforming it from minimal search visibility to prominent discoverability across branded Google Search results and AI-powered search experiences.

Industry
Personal Branding
Project Type

Role
Independent Consultant
Platform

Business Context

The client's LinkedIn profile had limited visibility across branded Google Search results and AI-powered search experiences despite having an established professional background. The objective was to improve profile positioning, strengthen keyword relevance, and increase discoverability through strategic profile optimisation.

My Contribution

  • Conducted a complete LinkedIn profile audit.
  • Rewrote the Headline and About section.
  • Optimised the Experience section.
  • Reorganised the Featured section.
  • Improved keyword relevance across the profile.
  • Strengthened profile positioning for better discoverability.

What Changed

Before Optimisation
  • • Profile had limited keyword optimisation.
  • • LinkedIn profile was not prominently discoverable in branded Google Search.
  • • The profile did not appear within AI-powered search experiences.
  • • Content performance had opportunities for improvement.
After Optimisation
  • • The LinkedIn profile achieved #1 visibility for the client's branded Google Search query based on the available verification evidence.
  • • The profile became discoverable within AI-powered search experiences.
  • • Profile positioning and keyword relevance were significantly strengthened.
  • • Content performance showed verified positive improvement.

Optimisation Deliverables

Profile Audit Headline Optimisation About Section Rewrite Experience Optimisation Featured Optimisation Keyword Optimisation Call-to-Action Optimisation

Project Outcome

Following profile optimisation, the client's LinkedIn profile progressed from limited branded search visibility to becoming discoverable across Google Search and AI-powered search experiences. Supporting evidence also demonstrates improved content performance together with the complete optimisation workflow.
